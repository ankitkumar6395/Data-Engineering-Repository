## 🎯 Day 1: Big Data Completed ✅

### 🐘 Q1. What is Big Data?

Big Data refers to large, complex, or rapidly generated datasets that require specialized technologies to store, process, and analyze efficiently.

### 📊 Q2. What are the 5 Vs of Big Data?

The 5 Vs are:

- Volume: Amount of data.
    
- Velocity: Speed at which data is generated and processed.
    
- Variety: Different types and formats of data.
    
- Veracity: Quality and reliability of data.
    
- Value: Useful information extracted from data.
    

### 📦 Q3. What is Volume in Big Data?

Volume refers to the amount of data generated and stored. For example, a social media platform may store billions of posts, images, and videos.

### ⚡ Q4. What is the difference between Volume and Velocity?

Volume measures how much data exists, whereas Velocity measures how quickly data is generated and processed.

### 🔀 Q5. What is Variety in Big Data?

Variety refers to the different types and formats of data, such as SQL tables, JSON files, images, videos, and audio files.

### 🛡️ Q6. What is Veracity in Big Data?

Veracity refers to the quality, accuracy, consistency, and reliability of data. Missing values, duplicate records, and incorrect information can reduce data quality.

### 📋 Q7. What is the difference between structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data?

- Structured data: Organized into rows and columns, such as SQL tables.
    
- Semi-structured data: Has organizational elements but does not follow a rigid table structure, such as JSON and XML.
    
- Unstructured data: Does not follow a predefined tabular structure, such as images, videos, and audio files.
    

### 🔄 Q8. What is the difference between batch processing and real-time processing?

Batch processing processes collected data in groups, such as generating a daily sales report.

Real-time processing processes data continuously as it arrives, such as detecting suspicious banking transactions.

### 🌍 Q9. What are some real-world applications of Big Data?

Big Data is used in:

- E-commerce: Product recommendations and customer behavior analysis.
    
- Banking: Fraud detection and transaction monitoring.
    
- Healthcare: Medical data analysis.
    
- Social media: Content recommendations and user engagement analysis.
    

### 🖥️ Q10. Why is distributed processing important in Big Data?

Distributed processing divides a large workload across multiple computers. It helps process large datasets efficiently, scale computing capacity, and improve fault tolerance.

# Day 1: Introduction to Big Data  Completed ✅
 
### Q1. What is Big Data?
Big Data refers to large, complex, or rapidly generated datasets that require specialized technologies and approaches for efficient storage and processing.
### Q2. What are the 5 Vs of Big Data?
Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity and Value.
### Q3. What is distributed processing?
Distributed processing divides a large computational workload among multiple machines or processing nodes.
### Q4. What is the difference between batch and real-time processing?
Batch processing processes collected data in groups, while real-time processing processes data continuously as it arrives.
### Q5. Give examples of Big Data technologies.
Hadoop, Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, Hive, and Apache Airflow are examples of a technology used in modern Big Data and data engineering systems.
### Q6. What is structured data?
Structured data follows a predefined schema, such as rows and columns in a relational database.
### Q7. What is semi-structured data?
Semi-structured data does not follow a traditional relational table structure but contains organizational information, such as JSON or XML.
### Q8. Why is Big Data important for data engineers?
Data engineers build systems that collect, store, transform, process, and deliver large-scale data for analytics and business applications
