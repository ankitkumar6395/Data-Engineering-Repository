# Day 1 — Introduction to ETL Data Engineering Completed ✅
 
### 1. What is ETL?  
ETL stands for Extract, Transform, and Load. It is a process used to extract data from sources, transform it, and load it into a destination system.
 
### 2. What happens during extraction?  
Data is collected from sources such as files, databases, APIs, or applications.
 
### 3. What happens during transformation?  
Data is cleaned, filtered, converted, standardised, and prepared for loading.
 
### 4. What happens during loading?  
The transformed data is stored in a destination such as a database or data warehouse.
 
### 5. Why is ETL important for Data Engineers?  
ETL allows data engineers to build reliable pipelines that move and prepare data for analysis and business applications.
